or join a room

How it works

  1. Create a room on one device.
  2. Open the link (or scan the QR code) on the other device.
  3. Send files and messages — they go directly device-to-device. The server only introduces the two devices; it never sees or stores your files.

Keep both devices on the page while a file is moving — nothing is stored anywhere in between.
